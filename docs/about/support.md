[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/wx-one/wxingress/discussions) and

development and gives you a direct line to the people who build it.

## Fast release channel

The fast release channel is run by the emergency response team of WX-ONE. When a critical vulnerability
affects wxingress, NGINX or a component wxingress ships, the team builds a targeted fix and makes it
available to fast release channel customers right away.

How this relates to the public releases:

- **Public releases are not held back.** There is no artificial delay for anyone. wxingress is usually
  released publicly within about 24 to 48 hours on working days after a fix is ready, as an official
  release for everyone.
- **Emergency releases are not official releases.** They are targeted fixes for critical
  vulnerabilities, built to close the gap until the next official release. They contain the fix and
  nothing else, and they are superseded by the official release that follows.
- What the channel buys you is the time in between: a fix for a critical vulnerability within hours,
  before the regular release process has run, and directly from the people who built it.

The package covers up to **10 clusters** of the legal entity. Each further 10 clusters cost €500 per month.
