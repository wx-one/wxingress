# Support

wxingress is open source and free to use. Community help happens in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/wx-one/wxingress/discussions) and
[issues](https://github.com/wx-one/wxingress/issues), on a best-effort basis.

If your clusters depend on wxingress, WizardTales offers commercial support. It funds the project's
development and gives you a direct line to the people who build it.

**Fast release channel.** Your organisation gets access to wxingress's fast release channel.

**Emergency support desk.** Everything in the fast release channel, plus an emergency support desk
during business hours (8×5) with prioritised handling of your requests. 24×7 coverage is available on
request.

All prices are per month and per legal entity. Each company of a group that uses wxingress needs its own
package.

## Ordering

Order by email to **[ingress-support@wx1.de](mailto:ingress-support@wx1.de)**. Please include the name and
address of the legal entity, the package you want, and a technical contact. For 24×7 coverage, tell us
what you need and we will send you an offer.

!!! note "Support is not a feature contract"
    A paid support package entitles you to the support described above. It does not entitle you to
    demand new features or changes to wxingress's roadmap. Feature requests from customers are welcome,
    and like every other request they go through the project's normal process.
